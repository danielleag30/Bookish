## D. KINDS OF BEING

The series has **no non-human character types**. Everyone named is human. Do not add a fae/vampire/dragon axis; there isn't one.

What actually differentiates characters is whether they are an **Heir** (a person with a magical gift) or ungifted, and which gift they hold. If node shape needs a driver, use that:

- **Heir** — has a recorded gift
- **Ungifted** — no gift attested
- **Shifter** — a gift subtype worth separating visually, since shifters spend page-time as animals (Messer as eyun, giant squid and leopard; Sven as panther)
- **Creature** — the eyun as a species. Note that Blue, the only eyun on the page, *is* Messer; there is no separate eyun character.

*Reason: Story Darlings lists Messer's forms explicitly and identifies Blue as Messer. Esta Pinto lists Blue as its own entry, which is the pre-reveal reading — do not create two nodes.*

## G. WHAT THE READER WRONGLY BELIEVED

This section is unusually large, and that is the defining feature of this book. The narrator withholds from the reader, not just from other characters.

| Kai's influence over Brynn is a violation she is unaware of | Ch. ~56 | She instructed Kai to use his influence visibly so Acker would believe her an innocent |
| Aurora, or possibly Kai, leads the Alaha rebellion | Ch. ~56 | Brynn leads it |

## K. CONFIDENCE AND GAPS

**Could not confirm:**

- Whether "Jovie" or "Jovinnia" is the canonical full name. SoBrief and the Ink and Imaginings guide both use Jovinnia as the formal name with Jovie as the diminutive; the Fandom wiki page title is "Brynn/Jovinnia." No publisher source uses either. Treated here as Jovinnia formal, Jovie in use.
- Where Brynn actually grew up. Esta Pinto and SoBrief say Kai's parents took her in. SuperSummary says Wren claims to have rescued her from an empty fishing boat. Story Darlings and SoBrief both place her living alone on Urchin Row at the start. Most likely raised by Wren's household and later moved to Urchin Row, but no source states the transition.
- Whether Wells and Olivia have gifts of their own. Every source describes them only via the matching bond. Left as "not attested" rather than guessed.
- Whether Grenadine has a gift. She is a former queen of a realm where the ruling families are Heirs, which implies one, but no source names it. Not filled in.
- Roles for Phillipe and Willard. They appear in one character list with no description at all. Excluded rather than invented.
- Anya (dressmaker) and Drago (missing man, with his son) have one-line roles but no attested scene presence. Excluded from the character table; Drago's disappearance is likely a thread rather than a character.
- The colour palette in section J is inferred from descriptions, not sampled from the cover.
- **Whether a book 3 exists, and what it would cover.** Publisher and retail copy uniformly says duology. The only counter-evidence is one blogger's paraphrase of an author Instagram reel. Even taking it at face value, it says nothing about whether Jovie is in book 3, so the shape of her arc is not established either. Both "the duology is complete" and "Jovie's story ends in book 2" are unsupported claims. Treat the series as open-ended-unknown in any UI copy.
- **Search hazard:** there is an unrelated series titled *Fire and Metal* by Daemona (*The Ember and the Blade*, *The Ember and the Shadow*, *The Ember and the Throne*). Any future search for "Fire and Metal book 3" will return it. Always search with the author name attached.

**Sources conflict:**

- **Vad.** Story Darlings calls him a Roison oracle who knew Acker in training. SoBrief calls him a Roison oracle and Acker's former army comrade who betrayed his battalion. Esta Pinto calls him "a soldier who deserted Kenta." These are probably compatible — a Kenta-trained deserter now operating in Roison — but "deserter" versus "Roison native" is a real difference and I have used the two-source version.
- **Beau's relation to Acker.** SuperSummary says "sister"; Esta Pinto, Story Darlings and SoBrief all say half-sister, and all place Greta as Beau's mother while Grenadine is Acker's. Half-sister is the majority and internally consistent reading.
- **Edmond's fate.** SuperSummary says Jovie severs Edmond's magic and leaves him alive at Acker's request. Story Darlings phrases it as her having to kill his magic *because* Acker refused. The Light Wielder jacket copy settles it: she took his magic and spared his life at Acker's request. Both facts are true; the sequencing in Story Darlings is loose.
- **Page count and publisher.** The 2024 self-published edition is 497pp under "Saturday Books" per one review blog; the 2025 Macmillan edition also lists Saturday Books as the imprint. Not a canon issue, but do not treat the two editions' page numbers as interchangeable.

**Genuinely ambiguous in the text:**

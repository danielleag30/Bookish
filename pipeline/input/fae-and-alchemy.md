## Book 1: Quicksilver

- Saeris Fane, a thief in the desert city of Zilvaren, hides that she is an alchemist
- Saeris scavenges the Undercroft for water and finds an enchanted gauntlet
- The gauntlet opens a portal and pulls Saeris into Yvelia, the Fae realm
- Saeris meets Kingfisher, a Fae warrior who wields shadow and carries shifting tattoos
- Carrion Swift, a rogue from Saeris's past in Zilvaren, is pulled through with her
- Saeris is revealed to be a quicksilver alchemist, the first in generations
- Kingfisher needs an alchemist to reforge the Fae army's weapons against the Sanasroth threat
- Everlayne, Kingfisher's sister, welcomes Saeris to the court at Cahlish
- Saeris and Kingfisher move from open hostility to a reluctant alliance
- Ren, Saeris's brother, remains behind in Zilvaren — her reason to want to return
- Saeris and Kingfisher are bound by a quicksilver bond neither of them chose

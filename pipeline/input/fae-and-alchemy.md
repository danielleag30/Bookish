## Book 1: Quicksilver

- Zilvaren is a harsh desert city on a human world, ruled by Madra, an immortal queen
- Saeris Fane is a thief in Zilvaren who hides that she can manipulate metal — an alchemist
- Hayden Fane, Saeris's younger brother, works as a store clerk in the seventh ward
- Iris Fane, their mother, is dead before the story opens
- Saeris scavenges the Undercroft for water and finds an enchanted Fae gauntlet
- The gauntlet opens a portal and pulls Saeris into Yvelia, the frozen Fae realm
- Carrion Swift, a smuggler and gambler from Zilvaren, is pulled through with her
- Saeris meets Kingfisher, a Fae warrior over 1,700 years old, Lord of Cahlish
- Kingfisher has been corrupted by long exposure to quicksilver
- Everlayne is Kingfisher's half-sister and a daughter of King Belikon
- Everlayne welcomes Saeris to the court at Cahlish, a fiefdom warded against Belikon
- Te Léna is a healer at Cahlish who explains Fae customs and mating bonds to Saeris
- Renfis Orithian commands the Yvelian army and is a friend to Kingfisher
- Lorreth is a Fae warrior and bard who fights alongside Kingfisher
- Belikon De Barra rules Yvelia as king from the Winter Palace
- Saeris is revealed to be a quicksilver alchemist, the first in generations
- Kingfisher needs an alchemist to reforge Fae weapons against the vampires of Sanasroth
- Malcolm is the vampire king of Sanasroth, a former Fae who took on the blood curse
- Belikon is revealed to have secretly allied with Malcolm for millennia, feeding his horde
- Lupo Proelia is the rebellion opposing Belikon; Danya, Korrix, Foley, Merelle and Vashgidyan fight in it
- Saeris and Kingfisher move from open hostility to a reluctant alliance
- Carrion Swift is revealed to be Carrion Daianthus, heir to the Yvelian throne
- Finran, Kingfisher's father, carried the infant Carrion to Zilvaren a thousand years ago to hide him from the usurper Belikon, glamouring his ears and canines so he would pass as human
- Rurik and Amelia Daianthus were the former king and queen of Yvelia, of Carrion's line
- Carrion's bloodline created the curse that made Malcolm a vampire, so no vampire can drink from him or enthrall him
- Onyx is a fox who attaches himself to the group
- Archer, a fire sprite, is head caretaker at Cahlish
- Edina, an oracle at Cahlish, dies
- Harron, commander of Madra's guard in Zilvaren, dies
- Malcolm mortally wounds Saeris, and she survives as half Fae, half vampire
- Saeris kills Malcolm and by that inherits his throne, becoming vampire queen of Sanasroth
- Taladius is a vampire lord of Sanasroth and the one who made Saeris what she is
- Saeris and Kingfisher are bound by a quicksilver bond neither of them chose
- Kingfisher had promised to bring Hayden back to Saeris, and fails to

## Book 2: Brimstone

- Saeris rules Sanasroth as queen of the Blood Court, having killed Malcolm
- She proves her claim before the Lords of Midnight by feeding on Kingfisher's blood
- Saeris establishes new laws protecting those the court previously fed on
- Taladius becomes Saeris's representative in the court
- Kingfisher returns to Zilvaren with Carrion rather than with Hayden, having kept his oath only in part
- Carrion learns that his last living relative in Zilvaren has died
- Carrion co-owns an underground vault in Zilvaren's Silver City, the Brigand's Bank, with a partner named Eric
- Vorath Shah is a vault breaker who is visibly afraid of Kingfisher
- Madra sends infected feeders out of Zilvaren
- Edina, Kingfisher's mother, is revealed to be a spirit trapped in the quicksilver, able to speak through it
- A rot spreads across the realms and costs the Fae a thousand warriors
- Ereth, a vampire lord, attempts an assassination and is killed by Kingfisher
- Kingfisher permanently gives up his healing magic to save Onyx
- Kingfisher tells Saeris he loves her and wants to marry her, and she says it back
- Their bond is held to supersede the traditional Fae wedding requirements
- Saeris searches Ammontraíeth for a forge
- Saeris is pulled into the quicksilver and confronted by Orious, King Belikon's right hand
- Hayden Fane remains in Zilvaren

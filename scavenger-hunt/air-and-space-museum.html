Before you go

  • Entry is free, but check for timed-entry passes. The Mall museum has been using free timed passes — book them online the week before so nobody gets turned away at the door.
  • The Space Shuttle is not at this museum. Discovery lives at the Udvar-Hazy Center in Chantilly, Virginia, about 45 minutes away. Worth its own day.
  • Galleries rotate. The museum has been renovating in stages, so a hall may be closed or an aircraft moved. Every stop below has a "Can't find it?" backup that still earns the letter.
  • Plan about 2–3 hours. Eight stops with four kids is roughly that. The order below walks a rough loop rather than crisscrossing.
  • Best kid room in the building: How Things Fly, where touching things is the point. Stop 5. Leave time.
  • Costs extra: the planetarium and the giant-screen theater. Everything in this hunt is free.
  • Museum manners: walking feet, indoor voices, hands off anything without a "please touch" sign. Museum guides love questions — bonus mission 10 is asking one.
